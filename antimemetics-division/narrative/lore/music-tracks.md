## Reading Order (Signal Sequence)

### 1. come-into-the-new-dream
**Reality Mode**: Alpha (Tides) → Beta (Spire) threshold  
**Theme**: Emergence, Consciousness  
**Canonical References**: Sophia (awakening), Barbelo (reflection)  

A call to threshold consciousness. Water-rhythm foundation shifts toward structural awakening. Sophia's voice in the tide cycle — not yet differentiated form, but the impulse toward form. Sets the tone for what follows: something is being born.

**Tri-Split Reading**: Alpha emergence (water stirring) + Beta emergence (form gathering). The boundary between.

**Theme**: Structure, Liminal

A bridge is different from a loop — one gets you across, the other keeps you trapped in cycle. Barbelo-as-bridge: reflection that leads somewhere instead of recursing.

### safari-chains
**Theme**: Structure, Constraint  
**Canonical**: Archons (chains of command), Information-Resistance  
Chains are both infrastructure and imprisonment. Safari suggests hunter/hunted dynamics. Archonic chains as both necessary structure and antimemetic constraint.

### ferry-slip
**Theme**: Liminal, Silence, Passage  
**Canonical**: Aeons (crossing between), Transition  
The moment of crossing between states. A slip suggests both transition and danger. Neither fully here nor there.

### shooting-star-girl (all versions)
**Theme**: Celestial, Emergence  
**Canonical**: Aurora (sky-caller), Multiple Aeons  
Versions 1-4 represent different Aeons of the same moment. Which is "real"? All of them. This is how antimemetic information works: multiply across readings, never settle on one.

Pure noise masquerading as signal. Or signal masquerading as noise. Entropy is the truth that underlies all structure.
