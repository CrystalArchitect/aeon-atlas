# Maintainers

This document describes the maintainers of the Aeon Atlas project and the stewardship model we follow.

## Stewardship Model

Aeon Atlas operates under a **stewardship-based governance model**. Maintainers are domain experts who take responsibility for specific areas of the codebase. This model emphasizes:

- **Expertise-driven leadership**: Maintainers are recognized for their deep knowledge and demonstrated capability in their domains
- **Non-hierarchical structure**: All maintainers are peers with equal voice in governance decisions
- **Transparent decision-making**: Discussions and decisions are visible to the community
- **Revocable responsibility**: Maintainers can step down at any time, and their status can be re-evaluated if they become inactive or fail to uphold community standards

## Becoming a Maintainer

We welcome contributions and recognize outstanding contributors by considering them for maintainer roles. To become a maintainer:

1. Make consistent, high-quality contributions to the project
2. Demonstrate deep understanding of your domain
3. Show commitment to the project's vision and values
4. Be nominated by an existing maintainer or core team member
5. Be approved by the core team

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers commit to:

- **Code review**: Review pull requests in their domain with care and timeliness
- **Guidance**: Mentor contributors and help them grow
- **Advocacy**: Represent the project and community
- **Technical decisions**: Help guide technical direction in their domain
- **Communication**: Keep the community informed about changes and decisions
- **Security**: Report and help address security issues promptly
- **Standards**: Uphold code quality, testing, and documentation standards

## Inactive Maintainers

Maintainers who have not participated for 6+ months may be moved to emeritus status. They can reactivate at any time by resuming contributions.

## Stepping Down

Maintainers can step down at any time by notifying the project. This is a normal part of open source development and we appreciate the contributions made while active.

## Decision-Making

- **Technical decisions**: Discussed in issues and pull requests; consensus among active maintainers
- **Governance changes**: Require discussion and approval from core team
- **Urgent matters**: Core team makes decisions with community notification

## Communication

- **GitHub Issues**: Feature requests and bug reports
- **Pull Request Reviews**: Technical discussion and feedback
- **Email**: For sensitive or private matters
