# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Aeon Atlas is committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive community for all participants, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, political beliefs, profession, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, socioeconomic status, or technical ability.

We are dedicated to creating an environment where all people feel safe, respected, and valued.

## Our Values

### Technical Honesty

We value accuracy and truth in all technical discussions:

- Present evidence-based arguments with sources
- Admit when you don't know something
- Correct errors promptly and gracefully
- Challenge claims respectfully with evidence

### Intellectual Rigor

We maintain high standards for technical work:

- Support claims with data and reasoning
- Write clear, maintainable code
- Review work carefully before sharing
- Continuously seek to improve your own work

### Collaborative Problem-Solving

We work together to overcome challenges:

- Listen to diverse perspectives
- Build on others' ideas
- Help others learn and grow
- Share knowledge generously

## Expected Behavior

All participants in Aeon Atlas are expected to:

- Be respectful and inclusive in all communications
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards other community members
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully
- Acknowledge and appreciate good work
- Ask questions rather than make assumptions
- Credit others for their contributions

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or exclusion based on any protected characteristic
- Deliberate deception or dishonesty
- Personal attacks or insults
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Threats of violence or intimidation
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Spam or off-topic behavior
- Deliberately disrupting the work of others

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying this code of conduct and taking appropriate corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

### Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the maintainers. You can:

- Open a private GitHub issue (mark it as confidential)
- Email the maintainers directly
- Reach out to any maintainer you trust

### Investigation and Response

Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially:

1. The team will investigate the incident
2. If substantiated, appropriate action will be taken
3. Possible actions include: private warnings, public requests for improved behavior, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from the community

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.0, and is used in many open source communities.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, etc.) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
